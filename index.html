About

Building with purpose, for people.

MHOBG Studio is the digital practice of Abdul Rahman Mohammed — a full-stack software developer, freelancer since 2016, and current Software Developer at SOTI. Based between Ghana and Canada, Abdul Rahman blends deep technical expertise with a passion for creating software that serves real human needs.

His journey began with web development in 2016 and grew into a versatile practice spanning AI integration, mobile apps, accessibility tools, and Islamic digital solutions. At SOTI, he led end-to-end delivery of Enhanced Remote Control, built the XSight Reports data pipeline, and drove test coverage from ~0% to ~80%.

Heavenly_Dev is the creative alias — MHOBG Studio is the vehicle that brings it all to life.
